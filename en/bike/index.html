Why Pamplona is one of Spain's best cycling destinations

Pamplona has become one of the most attractive cycling destinations in northern Spain thanks to its privileged location between the Pyrenees, the Camino de Santiago and the diverse landscapes of Navarra.

From here you can enjoy an extraordinary variety of rides:

  • Mountain climbs in the Pyrenees
  • Quiet country roads through traditional villages
  • Gravel routes across forests and natural parks
  • The famous Camino de Santiago
  • The spectacular Bardenas Reales desert landscape
  • The green valleys of Baztan and Roncal
  • Historic towns, castles and medieval villages

Many international cyclists choose to stay several nights in Pamplona because it allows them to explore completely different routes without changing accommodation every day.

Whether you are planning a weekend cycling break or a longer holiday in Navarra, Pamplona offers excellent food, modern services, bike shops, beautiful scenery and easy access to some of Spain's most rewarding cycling routes.

Ride into the Pyrenees

The western Pyrenees are one of Europe's classic cycling destinations.

From Pamplona you can easily reach legendary mountain roads, peaceful valleys and spectacular viewpoints that attract cyclists from all over the world.

Popular destinations include:

  • Roncesvalles
  • Isaba
  • Ochagavía
  • Belagua Valley
  • Larra
  • French border climbs

These routes combine demanding ascents with breathtaking scenery and quiet roads.

Discover the Irati Forest

The Irati Forest is one of the largest beech and fir forests in Europe.

Cyclists particularly enjoy its peaceful mountain roads, fresh air and beautiful natural surroundings.

Autumn is especially spectacular, although the area offers excellent riding conditions throughout much of the year.

Explore the Baztan Valley

The Baztan Valley offers gentle climbs, traditional Basque villages and beautiful green landscapes.

It is an excellent destination for cyclists looking for quieter roads and authentic rural scenery away from the busiest tourist routes.

The combination of rolling hills, historic architecture and excellent local cuisine makes it one of Navarra's hidden gems.

The unique landscapes of Bardenas Reales

For something completely different, head south towards the Bardenas Reales Natural Park.

This UNESCO Biosphere Reserve offers dramatic desert scenery unlike anywhere else in northern Spain.

Its quiet roads and wide-open landscapes make it an unforgettable destination for road cyclists, gravel riders and bikepacking enthusiasts.

Road cycling, gravel and bikepacking

Navarra is one of the few regions where different styles of cycling can be enjoyed within a short distance.

Road cyclists appreciate the smooth mountain roads and famous climbs.

Gravel riders can explore forest tracks, quiet rural lanes and natural parks.

Bikepacking travellers enjoy the extensive network of secondary roads linking villages, mountains and historic towns across the region.

Whatever your preferred style of riding, Pamplona is an ideal starting point.
