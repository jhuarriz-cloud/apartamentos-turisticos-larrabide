Looking for a comfortable and independent place to stay in Pamplona?

**Apartamentos Turísticos Larrabide** offers three fully equipped tourist apartments in a quiet and well-connected area of the city.

Our apartments are suitable for:

- Visitors exploring Pamplona and Navarra.
- Pilgrims walking the **Camino de Santiago**.
- Cyclists and bike tourists.
- Professionals travelling for work.
- Visitors attending congresses and events.
- Guests staying for several days.

The apartments are located at **Calle Larrabide 3 bis**, with easy access to the city centre and several important destinations.

They are a practical choice if you are visiting Pamplona for tourism, attending a congress at **Baluarte**, travelling for work or stopping on the **Camino de Santiago**.
